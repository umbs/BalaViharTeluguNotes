# TODO / Roadmap

Planned work for the Bala Vihar Telugu Notes. See [README.md](README.md) for the
current repository layout and build instructions.

The program has three levels (**Level 1**, **Level 2**, **Level 3**). Level 2 is
populated; Level 1 and Level 3 are still to be added as siblings under the same
structure. To add a level, create a `Level<N>/` folder mirroring `Level2/` (its
own `Textbook/`, `Workbook/`, `textbook.typ`, `workbook.typ`) with the entry
files pointing at `../template.typ`.

## Tasks

### Structure

1. **Create a Level 1 folder** — scaffold `Level1/` mirroring `Level2/` and
   point its entry files at `../template.typ`.

### Content (Level 1 & Level 2 curriculum)

2. **Telugu history** — add a little bit of the language's history to Level 1
   and Level 2.
3. **Literary stalwarts** — introduce prominent poets and writers of the
   language, both classical and modern, to some extent in L1 and L2.
4. **Well-known literary works** — cover some of the most famous literary works
   of the language, to some extent in the L1 and L2 curriculum.
5. **Satakams (overview)** — explain what "Satakams" are (100+ verse poems),
   highlighting prominent ones such as Vemana and Sumati.
6. **Teach famous Satakams** — include selected famous Satakams in L1 and L2.
7. **Children's padyalu** — add well-known children's poems (padyalu).
